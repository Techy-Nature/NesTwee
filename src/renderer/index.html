      Search folders

      A story formats folder holds one subfolder per format, each with a format.js (Twine 2 style) or header.html (Twine 1 style). Like Tweego, NesTwee also looks in storyformats folders in your home folder and next to the app, and in TWEEGO_PATH.

        NesTwee

        A desktop app that does everything Tweego does, with buttons instead of command-line flags: compile Twee to HTML, make Twine archives, decompile back to Twee, watch folders, add modules and head files.

        The compiler is a JavaScript port of Tweego by Thomas Michael Edwards (BSD 2-Clause). Bundled story formats keep their own licenses: SugarCube (BSD 2-Clause), Harlowe (Zlib), Chapbook (MIT), Snowman (MIT), Duplex (MIT), Paperthin (Zlib). OpenDyslexic by Abbie Gonzalez is bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

        Keyboard: Ctrl+B builds, Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- change text size.
